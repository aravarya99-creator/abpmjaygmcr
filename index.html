ℹ️ About Us
GMC & AH Rajouri — Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY
Ayushman Bharat — Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the world's largest health assurance scheme launched by Government of India. It provides health coverage of Rs 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization. At GMC & AH Rajouri, our dedicated PMAM team works to ensure every eligible beneficiary receives their entitled healthcare services seamlessly.
